When a settlement is unhappy, poor, besieged or starving, its people leave; when one is prosperous and welcoming, they arrive, inside a civilization and across borders. Emigration adds population migration and refugees to Civilization VII 1.5.0. Refugees flee wars and disasters, shelter in their own lands before they cross a border, and steer clear of a city under attack. Immigration stances and Open Borders shape the flows, and taking people in costs Gold and time. A departure abandons the tile those people worked and an arrival settles a new one, and people driven from their homes come back once it's calm. A foreign community that lasts puts down roots as a Cultural Enclave, a tile themed after its own people. A dashboard built around your settlements shows who is coming, who is leaving and why, with a forecast and what would stop it, and an Ethnic Composition lens and the Demographics mod show the rest. AI civilizations play the same system. Twelve languages. Best played with the Demographics mod.

Storytelling is a core purpose of this mod: the goal is to make migration pressures and demographic change legible as a human narrative, not only as systems and totals. The refugee and newcomer pop-ups quote the displaced themselves, in the words of each people's own refugees, exiles, and migrants. Each pop-up that asks for a decision has its own switch in the options.

A note: this mod abstracts migration and displacement into game systems, but the human reality behind them is anything but abstract. It is meant as a respectful acknowledgment of that reality, not to trivialize or profit from it. As part of releasing it I have donated to refugee and migrant charities (UNHCR, the IRC, Médecins Sans Frontières, IRAP, or a local resettlement group), and if you are able, please consider doing the same.
