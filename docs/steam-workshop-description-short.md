When a city is unhappy, poor, besieged, or starving, its people leave; when a city is prosperous and welcoming, they arrive, within and between civilizations. Emigration adds population migration and refugee systems to Civilization VII. Compatible with 1.5.0: it reads the reworked happiness stages, governments, and Celebrations, and is rebalanced so a city's economy matters alongside its happiness. Refugees flee wars and disasters, sheltering within their own lands before they cross a border; immigration stances and Open Borders shape the flows; and absorbing migrants carries a real, temporary cost. Population moves are real gameplay effects: a departure abandons the tile those people worked, and an arrival settles a new one. A foreign community that lasts puts down roots as a Cultural Enclave, a real tile themed after its own people. A full Migration dashboard, an Ethnic Composition map lens, and integration with the Demographics mod show you what is happening and why. Every leader and civilization in the game is tuned, and the whole interface is available in all 12 of the game's languages. Best played with the Demographics mod.

Storytelling is a core purpose of this mod: the goal is to make migration pressures and demographic change legible as a human narrative, not only as systems and totals. The refugee and newcomer pop-ups quote the displaced themselves, in the words of each people's own refugees, exiles, and migrants. Every pop-up that asks for a decision can be switched on or off in the options.

A note: this mod abstracts migration and displacement into game systems, but the human reality behind them is anything but abstract. It is meant as a respectful acknowledgment of that reality, not to trivialize or profit from it. As part of releasing it I have donated to refugee and migrant charities (UNHCR, the IRC, Médecins Sans Frontières, IRAP, or a local resettlement group), and if you are able, please consider doing the same.
