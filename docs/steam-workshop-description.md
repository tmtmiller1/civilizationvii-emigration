[h1]Emigration[/h1]
People leave settlements that fail them and move to ones that thrive, inside your civilization and across borders. A departure takes an outlying rural tile with it and an arrival settles a new one.
[h2]New in 4.0[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]A dashboard built around your settlements:[/b] who's arriving, who's leaving and why, what would keep them, and a forecast of who's likely to go next.
[*][b]Happiness by stage.[/b] Unhappy settlements lose people faster the unhappier they are. A Happy one's Happiness doesn't push anyone out.
[*][b]Dissuade newcomers[/b] for 1 Influence a turn.
[*][b]People come home[/b] on their own once home is calm, though some stay.
[*][b]Wars that make sense for refugees:[/b] quiet sieges stall, and refugees avoid cities under attack.
[/list]
Your saves and settings carry over from 3.6.
[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Compatible with 1.5.0.[/b]
[*][b]Prosperity.[/b] Settlements are scored on yields, happiness, war weariness and government, and people move toward stronger ones nearby.
[*][b]Refugees[/b] flee fighting, sieges, starvation, unrest and disasters, sheltering in their own civilization first.
[*][b]Cultural enclaves.[/b] A foreign community that lasts puts down roots as a tile themed after its own people.
[*][b]Policy cards,[/b] Open Borders and the cost of taking people in shape the flows.
[*][b]The AI plays by the same rules[/b] and at the same prices.
[*][b]Twelve languages.[/b]
[/list]

[h2]Caveats[/h2]
[list]
[*]Departures remove rural tile improvements by design.
[*]Single player only.
[*]A game saved with Emigration needs it to load. Adding it mid-game works.
[/list]
[h2]A note on the human reality behind this mod[/h2]
Migration and displacement are game systems; in life people leave home through war, persecution, disaster, or hardship. This mod aims to acknowledge, not trivialize, that.
I cannot sustain a per-subscriber pledge indefinitely, but I plan to mark major milestones with donations of time or money. Please consider supporting UNHCR, the IRC, Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF), IRAP, or local refugee and mutual-aid groups as you are able.
