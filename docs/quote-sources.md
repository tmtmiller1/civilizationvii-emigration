# Emigration — Historical Quote Sources

Every quote the mod shows, where it comes from, and how it was checked. The enclave decision shows one quote per
enclave; the refugee decision and the newcomers pop-up show one quote chosen by the civilization the people come
from; the call-our-people-home dialogs show one homecoming quote chosen by the player's own civilization. This file
is generated from the registries and the research notes; the registries are
`ui/emigration-quarter-bonuses.js` (enclaves), `ui/emigration-displaced-quotes.js` (refugees and migrants) and
`ui/emigration-return-quotes.js` (homecoming).

## Rules

- **Verified wording.** Every quote was located in a readable copy of its source, and the link below is where it
  was read. Quote-aggregator sites do not count. A modern historian's summary in quotation marks is not a quote.
  Cuts are marked with an ellipsis; a line is never extended past the point its notes name.
- **Translations.** A published translation names its translator. "trans." with no name is our own faithful
  translation of the verified original.
- **Voice.** Enclave quotes prefer the people's own writers, inscriptions, and proverbs. Refugee and migrant quotes
  prefer the words of an actual refugee, exile, or migrant of that people; then a witness who writes about them
  with dignity; otherwise the civilization has no row and a general pool quote is used.
- **Sensitivity.** No quote may generalize a people's character or pair them with a stereotype, frame the displaced
  as invaders, a burden, or a threat, quote a perpetrator of atrocities or a hate figure, trivialize sacred text,
  or dwell on graphic trauma.
- **Display.** The game's fonts cannot draw Arabic, Hebrew, Greek, Cyrillic, the Indic scripts, Thai, Ethiopic, or
  CJK, so for those the player sees only the English translation. The text rows keep the original.
- **Unmet civilizations.** A refugee or migrant from a civilization the player has not met gets a pool quote, so the
  quote never names who is coming.

Audited 2026-09-14, and again 2026-09-16 for the ten quotes of the civilizations added with the roster. "fixed"
means the wording, speaker, or citation was corrected against the source; "replaced" means the old quote was
unverifiable, misattributed, or failed the sensitivity rules. The note says why.

Two of the ten new quotes were drafted from memory and did not survive checking: the Herodotus Greek was a
reconstruction (it reads `μακρῷ`, not `πολλὸν`, in a different word order) and the Wang Geon line had
lost its opening `我`. Both were replaced with the transmitted text before shipping.

back, chosen by the player's own civilization: Egypt, French Empire, Great Britain, Greece, Han, Heian, Maurya, Ming,

public domain worldwide (translator dead over seventy years) or ours; two candidates were dropped for that reason
(Frost, North of Boston 1914; Gardiner's 1916 Sinuhe), and Halevi's Zion song, Mickiewicz's invocation, Pushkin's
revisit to the place of his exile and Hávamál 36 were set aside under the sensitivity and fit rules.
