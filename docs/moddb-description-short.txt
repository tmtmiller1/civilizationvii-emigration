Emigration brings migration and displacement into Civilization VII. People leave cities that are starving, unhappy or under siege and settle where life is better, at home or abroad. War and disaster force families to flee, and communities that hold on in a new land can put down roots as Cultural Enclaves.

Migration is a human experience before it's a game system, and the mod tries to treat it that way. Refugees and newcomers speak in its pop-ups, in the words of each people's own displaced, so the numbers read as stories. It's meant as a respectful acknowledgment, never to make light of displacement or profit from it, and alongside its release I've donated to refugee and migrant charities. If you're able, please consider doing the same.

- Refugees from war, siege, famine and disaster
- Cultural Enclaves themed after each people
- Policy cards, a Migration dashboard and map lenses
- 38 leaders and 50 civilizations, 12 languages. Best with Demographics.
